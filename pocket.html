Finger Pocket Designer

A mono, duo or three-finger pocket block for lifts and hangs, sized to your fingers. Your measurements stay on this device.

Unlevel edgeFinger pocketTalon grip
Start here
  1. Pick the fingers (Fingers): the width follows their widths from the Unlevel edge page's hand table; you can still type a width in (Pocket).
  2. Set the pocket: blind (a floor stops the fingertips, like a hangboard pocket) or right through; depth and rim radius like a hangboard pocket.
  3. Check the strength (below the 3D view): safety factor at least 2 at your heaviest pull.
  4. Export and print it flat, no supports. Hang it from the cord; the fingers go in from the rounded face and pull on the bar.

Export

The STL is the plain part. The 3MF files also carry layer height, walls, infill and top/bottom layers. PrusaSlicer and OrcaSlicer keep your current printer, nozzle and filament. Bambu Studio only applies the settings when the file names its printer presets, so pick your Bambu printer.
