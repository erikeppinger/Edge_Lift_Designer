Talon Grip Designer

A round grip for the open-hand claw (lumbricals): fingertips spread over a dome that narrows towards the load.

Unlevel edgeFinger pocketTalon grip
Start here
  1. Pick a shape (Shape): the tapered talon grip or a printable ball, then adjust the diameter to your hand and the taper to how hard it should be.
  2. Set the cord: hole and knot pocket for your cord.
  3. Export and print it standing on its foot, no supports. Thread the cord up through the foot, knot it in the pocket at the top, hang the load from the cord.

The STL is the plain part. The 3MF files also carry layer height, walls, infill and top/bottom layers. PrusaSlicer and OrcaSlicer keep your current printer, nozzle and filament. Bambu Studio only applies the settings when the file names its printer presets, so pick your Bambu printer.
